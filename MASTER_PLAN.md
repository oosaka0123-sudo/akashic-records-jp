# Master Plan

Goal: Build the clearest Japanese educational site about Akashic Records.

## Positioning

The site distinguishes historical facts, source claims, modern interpretations, personal beliefs, and scientific evidence.

## Main areas

- Beginner guide
- History and origins
- Key people
- Concepts
- Reading practices
- Science and psychology
- Religion and philosophy comparisons
- Sources and archive
- Glossary
- FAQ

## Product goals

- Mobile first
- Fast, accessible pages
- Clear citations and update dates
- Scalable to 100+ articles
- No unverified AI-generated claims

## Phase 1

1. Define information architecture
2. Select technical stack
3. Build homepage and article template
4. Publish 10 cornerstone articles
5. Add structured data, sitemap, robots and analytics readiness

## Phase 2

Expand topic clusters, timeline, people map, glossary and source archive.
